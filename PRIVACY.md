# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection

VoiceClone Studio Pro collects the following data:

### Voice Recordings
- **What**: Audio recordings of your voice
- **Purpose**: Creating your personal voice profile
- **Storage**: Processed locally in browser; sent to voice provider only if configured
- **Retention**: Until you delete them

### Voice Profile
- **What**: Mathematical representation of your voice characteristics
- **Purpose**: Generating speech in your voice
- **Storage**: Local browser storage or cloud provider
- **Retention**: Until you delete the profile

### Generated Audio
- **What**: Speech audio generated from your text
- **Purpose**: Your use case (personal, professional)
- **Storage**: Local browser memory
- **Retention**: Until you delete or download

### Settings & Preferences
- **What**: Application configuration
- **Purpose**: Customizing your experience
- **Storage**: Local browser storage
- **Retention**: Until you clear browser data

## What We Don't Collect

- We do NOT collect personal identification information
- We do NOT track your browsing behavior
- We do NOT share data with third parties (unless you configure a cloud provider)
- We do NOT use your voice data for any purpose other than what you explicitly request

## Third-Party Providers

If you configure a cloud voice provider (e.g., ElevenLabs):
- Your recordings may be sent to their servers for processing
- Review their privacy policy before connecting
- You control when and what data is sent

## Your Rights

### Access
You can view all your data within the application at any time.

### Deletion
You can permanently delete:
- Individual recordings
- Voice profiles
- Generated audio
- All data at once

### Portability
You can download:
- Generated audio files
- Dataset quality reports
- Voice profile metadata

## Consent

Before creating a voice profile, you must confirm:
> "I confirm that I own this voice or have authorization to create this voice profile."

This consent is recorded and required for profile creation.

## Data Security

- All data is processed securely
- No data is stored without encryption in transit
- Deletion is permanent and complete
- No backups of deleted data are retained

## Changes to This Policy

Any changes to data handling will be communicated through the application.

## Contact

For privacy concerns, please refer to the application's help section.
